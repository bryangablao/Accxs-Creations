# 🏢 Accxs-Creations

**Accxs-Creations** is a company that creates websites. We design and build modern, easy-to-use websites and web apps, from personal portfolios to games, streaming sites, and social apps.

🔗 **Visit the website:** https://bryangablao.github.io/Accxs-Creations

## 📖 Overview

Accxs-Creations turns ideas into working websites. Whether you need a personal portfolio, a business page, a game, or a full web app, we build it to fit your needs, with a clean design that works on every device.

This website is the home of Accxs-Creations. It introduces who we are, what we build, and how to get your own website made.

## 🎯 Purpose

The goals of Accxs-Creations are to:

- **Build websites** that are modern, fast, and easy to use
- **Bring ideas to life** by creating custom websites based on what each person or business needs
- **Showcase our work** through real projects that people can visit and use
- **Make web presence accessible** so anyone can have a professional website

## 🛠️ Services

- 🌐 **Website creation** – customized websites built to your needs
- 👤 **Personal portfolios** – a professional page to present yourself and your work
- 🎮 **Games and entertainment sites** – browser games and streaming-style websites
- 💬 **Social and web apps** – interactive apps with posting, messaging, and more
- 📱 **Responsive design** – every site works on phones, tablets, and computers

Websites are typically ready to go live within 1–2 weeks, depending on the project.
