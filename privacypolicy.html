1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled through Accxs Creations websites, contact forms, project quote requests, email communications, portfolio inquiries, software or website development discussions, and related online services that link to this Policy. It does not automatically govern third-party websites, platforms, payment processors, hosting providers, or services that maintain their own privacy practices.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of information:

Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, and contact details.
  • Project type, project requirements, requested features, budget or timeline details you choose to share.
  • Messages, files, feedback, support questions, and business information sent to us.
  • Billing or transaction-related details where a third-party processor is used. We may receive transaction status but do not necessarily receive full payment-card data.

Technical Information

  • IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and approximate location derived from network information.
  • Pages viewed, referring pages, timestamps, clicks, and other usage or diagnostic information.
  • Cookie identifiers, local storage preferences, and analytics information when enabled.

3. How We Use Information

Respond to inquiries and quote requests.
Plan, design, develop, test, deliver, and maintain projects.
Provide customer service and project communications.
Secure, troubleshoot, and improve our website and services.
Maintain business records and prevent fraud or abuse.
Comply with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Cookies, Local Storage, and Analytics

Our website may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to remember preferences such as light or dark mode, support essential functionality, understand site performance, and improve user experience. Third-party services embedded in or linked from our website may set their own cookies according to their policies.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose limited information to service providers or contractors that help us operate our website, host projects, process communications, manage forms, analyze site performance, or deliver contracted work. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or complete a business reorganization. Providers are expected to handle information only for the services they perform.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including responding to inquiries, fulfilling projects, providing support, maintaining business and tax records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable requirements. When data is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymize, or securely archive it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission, email system, hosting platform, or storage method is completely secure. You should avoid sending passwords, full payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary contact forms or email unless specifically requested through a secure channel.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to personal information, request correction or deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, request data portability, or lodge a complaint with an appropriate authority. These rights may be subject to legal exceptions and identity verification.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended primarily for businesses, professionals, creators, and individuals seeking digital services. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in a manner that would require parental consent under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. International Users and Data Transfers

Accxs Creations may work with clients or service providers in different countries. As a result, information may be processed in jurisdictions other than the country where you live. Where required, we take reasonable steps intended to support lawful handling of personal information across borders. Privacy protections can differ by jurisdiction.

11. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to Google Maps, social platforms, hosting services, code repositories, payment processors, form services, or other third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review their terms and privacy notices before providing personal information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our services, technology, business practices, or legal obligations. When we make material changes, we may update the effective date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Continued use of the website after an update means the revised Policy will apply to future interactions, subject to applicable law.
